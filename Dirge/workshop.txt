title=Requiem of the Dead: Dirge
description=[h1]Requiem of the Dead: Dirge[/h1]
description=
description=The Dirge mod introduces specialty zombies to the world, each with their own aura, behavior, and danger level. These infected are meant to add unpredictability, pressure, and a little extra dread to encounters that might otherwise feel routine.
description=
description=[b]If you see a glowing aura around a zombie, pay attention. It is probably not just there to look pretty.[/b]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Screamers[/h2]
description=[i]Aura Color: Purple[/i]
description=
description=Screamers will let out a scream when players get within a certain range. That scream can:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Draw zombies from the surrounding area
description=[*]Spawn additional zombies if none are nearby
description=[*]Disorient nearby players for a set number of seconds
description=[/list]
description=
description=A Screamer may not be the strongest infected on its own, but ignoring one can turn a manageable situation into a very loud, very hungry problem.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]EMPs[/h2]
description=[i]Aura Color: Teal[/i]
description=
description=EMPs release an EMP pulse when they die. This pulse can:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Attract zombies from a large radius
description=[*]Knock down players who are very close to the EMP when it goes off
description=[*]Cause nearby players in the outer pulse wave to stumble
description=[*]Drain a small amount of endurance from affected players
description=[/list]
description=
description=EMPs also behave the way you would expect around electronics and vehicles. If one goes off near something powered, mechanical, or electronic, expect trouble.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Juggernauts[/h2]
description=[i]Aura Color: Light Blue[/i]
description=
description=Juggernauts are tougher than ordinary zombies. They are:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Stronger
description=[*]Harder to hit
description=[*]Able to take more damage
description=[/list]
description=
description=The worse part is that Juggernauts can [b]share this power with nearby zombies[/b]. Any zombie empowered by a Juggernaut will share the same light blue aura. The buff ends when the Juggernaut itself is killed.
description=
description=So, if a whole group suddenly looks stronger, meaner, and harder to put down, find the Juggernaut. It is the rotten little battery powering the problem.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Devourers[/h2]
description=[i]Aura Color: Green — Triggered Scavenger Aura: Dark Blue[/i]
description=
description=There are two types of Devourers: [b]Gluttons[/b] and [b]Scavengers[/b]. You will not know which one you are looking at until you attack it.
description=
description=[h3]Gluttons[/h3]
description=
description=Gluttons do exactly what the name suggests. They eat. Players. Corpses. Their own kind. It does not matter to them. They will consume whatever they can get to, and when they eat, they eat [i]everything[/i] — including clothing, tools, weapons... you name it.
description=
description=[h3]Scavengers[/h3]
description=
description=Scavengers are similar to Gluttons, but attacking them can be dangerous. When struck, a Scavenger's aura will turn dark blue. Once triggered, it will:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Self-buff like a Juggernaut
description=[*]Scream like a Screamer
description=[/list]
description=
description=That scream will draw zombies from the surrounding area, but it will [b]not[/b] spawn additional ones.
description=
description=Sometimes you may come across a Devourer that is already dark blue. This means it is already in its buffed state.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Boss Zombies[/h2]
description=[i]Aura Color: Gold[/i]
description=
description=Boss Zombies are [b]admin-spawned only[/b]. They have the abilities of all the specialty zombies listed above. Unlike regular EMPs, their EMP pulse does not require them to die first.
description=
description=If you see gold, assume the situation has become deeply unfortunate.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Customization & Admin Tools[/h2]
description=
description=Every aspect of Dirge is customizable, including:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Spawn weights
description=[*]Spawn chances
description=[*]Spawn distributions
description=[*]Ability timing and ranges
description=[/list]
description=
description=Admins can also convert ordinary zombies into specialty zombies through the context menu. Right-click near a zomboid and select which type you would like to convert it into.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
