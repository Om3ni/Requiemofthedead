/*
    THE SCAVENGER'S TWO SKINS. It converts into RQ_Scavenger - the Devourer
    biped with an emerald core, visually identical to the Glutton, which is

    RQ_ScavengerEnraged, the same biped with the crimson core. The biped item
    is shared by both outfits; only the core changes, so the flip is one
    persistent-outfit write on the server and one targeted repaint.

    THE GLUTTON WEARS ITS OWN TWIN of this biped (RQ_GluttonBiped, same model
    and texture under a separate item and GUID) because clothing defence is
    a property of the item: arming the Scavenger must not arm the Glutton.

    THE SCAVENGER'S ARMOUR, at the default ZombiesArmorFactor of 2.0:
      melee  = (0.5*23.33 + 23.33) * 2.0 = 69.99
      ranged = (1.5 * 26.67)       * 2.0 = 80.01
    Deliberately SHORT of the Juggernaut's 85/85. The Scavenger is dangerous
    for what it does to the zombies around it, not for how long it stands,
    and collapsing the two would erase the ladder the lab measured (E12:
    Glutton one hit, Scavenger three, Juggernaut five). Both numbers sit
    under the cap so a host's own dials still move them. The engine chain,
    the BloodLocation gate and the sums-across-items rule are cited in
    RQ_Juggernaut.txt and apply here unchanged.
*/
