description=[h2]What is inside[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]RFTDCore[/b] - the harness everything else stands on: unified logging, networking, capability-based staff gates, anti-grief. No gameplay of its own.
description=[*][b]Dragonfly[/b] - the admin command center. Tabbed panel on a keybind (default Shift+U); siblings bolt their own tabs on.
description=[*][b]RFTDDragonfly[/b] - the deck. Dragonfly reborn in new chrome, incubating alongside the classic panel. Presentation only.
description=[*][b]RFTDStaffTools[/b] - grant or revoke individual capabilities on the built-in roles, plus an extended ESC scoreboard.
description=[*][b]RFTDMemoir[/b] - a craftable journal that records a character's skills, traits and recipes, then recalls them onto whoever you respawn as.
description=[*][b]RFTDBanBox[/b] + [b]BBLibrary[/b] - server-side item ban engine; banned types stop spawning. BanBox ships with zero bans, BBLibrary carries the RotD list.
description=[*][b]RFTDReclamation[/b] - vehicle claims, locks, permissions and blowtorch dismantling, plus the Janitor that returns abandoned cars to the pool.
description=[*][b]RFTDDirge[/b] - specialty zombies, each with its own aura: Screamers, EMPs, Juggernauts, Devourers, and admin-only Bosses.
description=[*][b]RFTDHusbandry[/b] - taming, breeding, hutches and lifespans. Animals do not die of hunger or thirst.
description=[*][b]RFTDLastRites[/b] - client-side survival HUD for severe cold, severe heat and bleeding. Safe to add or remove at any time.
description=[*][b]RFTDReaper[/b] - detects vanilla zombie blooms and culls the excess before they spiral. Retires if 42.20's cull fix holds.
description=[*][b]RFTDLimes[/b] - one zone substrate for the whole family: named regions that decide how hard the world bites there. Substrate release.
description=[*][b]RFTDOddsAndEnds[/b] - small modules, each with a kill switch: RIPIT, Sticky Headwear, Quiet Horn, Lumberjack, Action Speed, Container Order, Inventory Collapse, Bookmark.
description=[/list]
