description=[h1]Requiem of the Dead[/h1]
description=
description=The complete Requiem of the Dead mod family in one Workshop item. Subscribe once; enable the mods your server wants. Everything updates together, so the family's versions always match.
description=
description=[h2]Install and load order[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Subscribe once. All fifteen mod IDs arrive together.
description=[*][b]Enable RFTDCore and load it first.[/b] Nothing else works correctly without it.
description=[*]Enable whichever siblings you want; their order relative to each other does not matter.
description=[*]Keep the family near the bottom of your load order, so Core's patches land on top of the mods they target.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h1]RFTDCore[/h1]
description=
description=The harness everything else stands on. No gameplay content of its own - it is the plumbing.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Unified server-side logging and a season record
description=[*]Networking helpers, rate limiting, and a default-deny command registry
description=[*]Capability-based staff access gates shared by every sibling mod
description=[*]Anti-grief: sledgehammer demolition restricted to safehouses
description=[*]Narrow compatibility fixes for third-party mods - if you do not run them, they cost you nothing
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Dragonfly[/h1]
description=
description=The admin command center: one tabbed panel to run the server from, that the rest of the family bolts its own controls onto.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Tabbed admin panel on a keybind (default Shift+U)
description=[*]Player management and live server state
description=[*]Item editor and console/log view
description=[*]Longstrider map and tour tools, plus a coordinates HUD
description=[*]Debug-menu gate (default F10) to hide the admin-only right-click clutter
description=[*]Sandbox-tiered and Admin-only by default
description=[/list]
description=
description=Siblings register their own tabs, so staff get one window instead of five - and run standalone if you leave Dragonfly off.
description=
description=[h1]RFTDStaffTools[/h1]
description=
description=Grant or revoke individual capabilities directly on the built-in roles - User, Moderator, Admin - with no custom roles and no reassigning players.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Extended ESC scoreboard with capability-gated per-row actions
description=[*]Direct editing of the built-in roles
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]RFTDMemoir[/h1]
description=
description=Death is permanent. Memory does not have to be. A craftable journal that records a character's skills, traits and recipes, then recalls them onto whoever you respawn as.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Bound to its owner, so a stolen journal will not rebuild a stranger
description=[*]Server-tunable XP restore, global or per-skill
description=[*]Traits, profession and recipes always return in full
description=[*]Staff restore action in the Dragonfly panel
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]RFTDBanBox + BBLibrary[/h1]
description=
description=A neutral, server-side item ban engine. Banned types are stripped from loot distribution at server start, so no new copies spawn. Items already in the world are left alone.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Optional confiscate-on-login, with a player message and a staff audit line
description=[*]Never an automatic punishment - the engine reports, it does not judge
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]BanBox ships with zero bans.[/b] Enable [b]BBLibrary[/b] for the RotD default list, or build your own: call [i]DFBanBox.ban("Module.Item")[/i] from any server-side mod. Example file included.
description=
description=[h1]RFTDReclamation[/h1]
description=
description=A vehicle lifecycle suite for dedicated multiplayer: who owns a car, who may drive it, and what happens when its owner never comes back.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Claim and lock vehicles, with granular per-player permissions
description=[*]Personal fleet panel; claims release themselves on inactivity
description=[*]Blowtorch dismantling for worn cars and wrecks - loot always drops
description=[*]Optional engine-salvage lock, for servers that want engines scarce
description=[*]Sandbox-gated staff vehicle spawner and a server-side audit ledger
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]The Janitor[/b] returns abandoned vehicles to the respawn pool on one generous rule: abandonment is a property of the player, not the object. If you log in, your things are yours. Wrecks are never touched.
description=
description=[h1]RFTDDirge[/h1]
description=
description=Specialty zombies, each with its own aura and behavior. If you see a glowing aura, pay attention.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Screamers (purple)[/b] - scream on approach, drawing zombies from the area or spawning them if none are near, and disorienting players
description=[*][b]EMPs (teal)[/b] - pulse on death: knockdown, stumble, endurance drain, and trouble for anything powered or mechanical nearby
description=[*][b]Juggernauts (light blue)[/b] - tougher and harder to hit, and they share that buff with nearby zombies until the Juggernaut itself dies
description=[*][b]Devourers (green)[/b] - Gluttons eat everything, gear included. Scavengers look identical until struck, then turn dark blue, self-buff and scream
description=[*][b]Bosses (gold)[/b] - admin-spawned only, carrying every ability above, and their pulse does not wait for death
description=[/list]
description=
description=Spawn weights, chances, distributions and every ability's timing and range are tunable. Admins can convert ordinary zombies from the context menu.
description=
description=[h1]RFTDHusbandry[/h1]
description=
description=Taming, breeding and day-to-day animal management, plus one small mercy.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Taming and breeding, with the pairing checks to make it work
description=[*]Hutch management, bedding, and hay as a proper timed action
description=[*]An Animals tab in the admin panel
description=[*]Lifespan handling, so herds behave over the long run
description=[*]Animals do not die of hunger or thirst
description=[/list]
description=
description=That last line is deliberate, not a realism change: it stops players finding dead livestock purely because somebody woke the cell up. Animals still die from disease and injury.
description=
description=[h1]RFTDLastRites[/h1]
description=
description=A client-side survival HUD: over-head and sidebar warnings the moment you hit a state that can kill you - severe cold, severe heat, bleeding.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Over-head indicators and sidebar moodles, with settings on the vanilla Client menu
description=[*]Four stages of escalation, from onset to actively dying
description=[*]A few hundred first-person thought lines as the cold or the heat takes hold
description=[*][b]Client-side only.[/b] Safe to add or remove at any time
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]RFTDReaper[/h1]
description=
description=A population fix for the vanilla duplication issue that makes groups of zomboids "bloom" into much larger hordes. Reaper detects blooms and removes the excess before they spiral.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Newborn, tile, outfit, wandered-birth-tile and outfit-cluster scans
description=[*]Removals paced across ticks, so a cull never becomes a network burst
description=[*]Tunable thresholds and cull logs with teleport-friendly coordinates
description=[/list]
description=
description=Areas may briefly feel thin; they refill on the normal respawn cycle. Overall population is not lowered.
description=
description=[b]Status - pending the 42.20 verdict.[/b] 42.20's headline fix is multiplayer zombie culling, the exact behavior this mod works around. If it holds up, Reaper retires - do not build a dependency on this mod id.
description=
description=[h1]RFTDOddsAndEnds[/h1]
description=
description=Small, self-contained modules, each with its own kill switch.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Reliquary[/b] - staff-placed, whitelist-gated stash for one named player; dissolves once emptied
description=[*][b]RIPIT[/b] - Rip All on the right-click menu, across open containers and no further
description=[*][b]Sticky Headwear[/b] - worn hats stay on when you take a hit
description=[*][b]Lumberjack[/b] - reversible weight scaling for wooden items, plus a one-click tree sweep
description=[*][b]Action Speed[/b] - per-family timed-action scaling
description=[*][b]Container Order[/b] - reorder your inventory sidebar buttons, and have it stick
description=[*][b]Inventory Collapse[/b] - fold Equipped and Hotbar out of the list, independently
description=[*][b]Bookmark[/b] - optional public-domain quote on login. Client-side, off by default
description=[/list]
description=
