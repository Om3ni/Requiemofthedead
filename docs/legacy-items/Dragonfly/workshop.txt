title=Requiem of the Dead: Dragonfly
description=[h1]Requiem of the Dead: Dragonfly[/h1]
description=
description=Dragonfly is the admin command center for the Requiem of the Dead mod family. It takes the buried, half-locked power of Project Zomboid's built-in admin tools, drags it into the light, and gives staff a single tabbed panel to run the server from - then lets the rest of the RFTD mods bolt their own controls onto it.
description=
description=Think of it as the dashboard. The other mods are the engine; Dragonfly is where you steer.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]The Admin Panel[/h2]
description=
description=A tabbed admin panel opened with a keybind (default Shift+U), plus an extension to the ESC scoreboard so player management is never more than a glance away. From one place, staff can:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Manage players, roles, and permissions
description=[*]Read live server state instead of guessing
description=[*]Reach the per-row actions that sibling mods register
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Unlocked Role Editor[/h2]
description=
description=Vanilla makes you build custom roles and reassign every player just to change what a Moderator is allowed to do. Dragonfly unlocks the built-in role editor so admins can grant or revoke individual capabilities directly on the roles that already exist - User, Moderator, Admin, and the rest. No custom roles, no reassigning, no fuss.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]One Panel for the Whole Family[/h2]
description=
description=When other Requiem of the Dead mods are installed alongside Dragonfly, they register their own tabs and per-row actions right into the panel:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Reaper[/b] - review and manage zombie population blooms
description=[*][b]Husbandry[/b] - keep an eye on livestock
description=[*][b]Dirge[/b] - manage and convert specialty zombies
description=[/list]
description=
description=Each of those mods still works perfectly well on its own. Dragonfly just gives them a shared home - and gives your admins one window instead of five.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]The Memoir[/h2]
description=
description=Death in the apocalypse is permanent. Memory does not have to be. The Memoir is a craftable journal that records a character - their skills, traits, and recipes - and recalls it onto whoever you respawn as. Write to it while you live; read it after you die, and the survivor you built comes back.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Snapshots skills, traits, and recipes
description=[*]Bound to its owner, so a stolen journal will not rebuild a stranger
description=[*]Server-tunable XP restore, from a full 100% down to whatever fraction your server prefers - traits, profession, and recipes always return in full
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Anti-Grief[/h2]
description=
description=Dragonfly also folds in the old Ladybug fix: sledgehammer demolition is restricted to safehouses, so nobody flattens the neighborhood out of spite. One less mess for staff to clean up.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Timed-Action Speed Scaling[/h2]
description=
description=Grind less, play more. Dragonfly can speed up the slow, repetitive timed actions on a per-family basis - pick anything from Vanilla up to Instant for each category independently:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Reading[/b] - books and maps
description=[*][b]Foraging[/b] - searching the ground
description=[*][b]Cleaning[/b] - blood, burns, and graffiti; washing yourself and clothing
description=[*][b]Repair[/b] - clothing, vehicle engines, and light bars
description=[*][b]Dismantle[/b] - breaking items down
description=[/list]
description=
description=Speeds run Vanilla / 10x / 20x / 30x / 40x / Instant. It is server-authoritative, so in multiplayer the speed-up is real - not just a progress bar that races ahead of the work. Inventory transfers are left untouched on purpose. Every family is its own sandbox option, so you tune exactly what fits your server.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Debug Menu Gate[/h2]
description=
description=Admins and moderators normally see Project Zomboid's debug-styled right-click options at all times, cluttering every context menu. Dragonfly hides them behind a single keypress (default F10): press to reveal the tools when you actually need them, press again to tuck them away.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Item BanBox[/h2]
description=
description=A neutral, server-side ban engine. Banned item types are stripped from loot distribution at server start, so no new copies spawn - prevention, not a destructive world sweep. Optionally (a sandbox toggle), any banned item a player is carrying is also removed when they log in, with a message to the player and an audit line for staff - never an automatic punishment.
description=
description=The engine ships with ZERO bans of its own - the ban list is supplied separately:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Enable the bundled [b]BBLibrary[/b] mod to apply the Requiem of the Dead default list, or
description=[*]Build your own - drop a Lua file into any server-side mod that calls [i]DFBanBox.ban("Module.Item")[/i] (see the example file in BBLibrary).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
