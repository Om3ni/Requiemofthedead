description=[h1]Requiem of the Dead: Reaper[/h1]
description=
description=The Reaper Mod is a population fix mod added to help with the zombie duplication issue that has been causing groups of zomboids to suddenly "bloom" into much larger hordes. These blooms were creating problems for both playability and server performance. A group that should have been manageable could suddenly become much larger than intended, not because of natural migration or respawn, but because of a duplication issue.
description=
description=Reaper is designed to detect those duplicated zombie blooms and remove the excess before they spiral into something worse. You may still notice some duplicated zomboids here and there, but the massive sudden horde growth should be greatly reduced.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]What Players May Notice[/h2]
description=
description=Because of how the fix works, you may occasionally come across areas that feel thinner than usual, or even temporarily empty. These areas should be:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Rare
description=[*]Relatively small
description=[*]Temporary
description=[*]Not permanent population changes
description=[/list]
description=
description=Those areas will naturally fill back in through the normal respawn cycle, though it may take a day or two. This does not mean the server population has been lowered overall.
description=
description=[b]Reaper is not meant to make the world safer.[/b] It is meant to stop broken population behavior from turning into glitch-haunted meat parades.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]What Reaper Does[/h2]
description=
description=Reaper detects and culls vanilla [i]PZPopMan64[/i] twin-spawn blooms. In simpler terms, it watches for situations where the game appears to create duplicated zombie groups that should not exist, then removes the duplicated excess.
description=
description=Reaper uses multiple layers of detection, including:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Newborn watcher detection
description=[*]Tile and outfit scans
description=[*]Wandered birth-tile scans
description=[*]Outfit-cluster scans for dispersed blooms
description=[/list]
description=
description=This allows it to catch both obvious duplication and more spread-out bloom behavior after zombies begin moving.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]Admin & Technical Details[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Server-side only[/b] — no client install required
description=[*][b]Tunable thresholds[/b] via sandbox options, so staff can adjust how aggressively it detects and removes duplicate population blooms
description=[*][b]Cull logs[/b] include teleport-friendly coordinates, making it easier for staff to review problem areas, check affected locations, and tune the system as needed
description=[/list]
